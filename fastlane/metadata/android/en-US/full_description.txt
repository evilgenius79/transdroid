Manage the torrents on your home server or seedbox from your Android device.

Transdroid connects to the torrent client you already run:

* Transmission
* qBittorrent (4.1+ and 5.x)
* rTorrent (including ruTorrent seedboxes)
* Deluge (1.3 and 2.x)

Features:

* View, filter, sort and manage running torrents; start, pause and remove them
* Per-file progress and download priorities
* Add torrents via magnet links, .torrent files, URLs, RSS feeds or Torznab search (Jackett/Prowlarr)
* Labels/categories from your client, shown as filters
* Optional notifications when torrents finish, and a home screen widget
* Automatic discovery of torrent clients on your local network
* Adaptive layout for phones, foldables and tablets

Your server credentials are stored encrypted with a hardware-backed key and never leave your device. Self-signed HTTPS servers are supported by pinning their certificate.
